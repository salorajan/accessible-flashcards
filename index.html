Quick Start Guide

  • Spacebar: Press once to hear the question, press again to hear the answer.
  • 'Y' / 'N': After hearing the answer, press Y if you were right or N if you were wrong.
  • 'R': Press to repeat the current question or answer.
  • 'Q': Press to finish your session and hear your total score.
