Subject: Free Accessible Flashcard App for the Visually Impaired

Hello,

I wanted to share "Accessible Flashcards," a tool designed specifically for blind and visually impaired students and their families. It is fully keyboard-driven and uses high-quality text-to-speech.

You can access the project and start studying here:
https://salorajan.github.io/accessible-flashcards/

Interactive Web App Features:
- "Test App" Button: Start immediately with sample SAT Math cards.
- "Upload JSON" Button: Load your own custom study sets.
- "Space" Button: Hear the question or answer.
- "Y / N" Buttons: Mark your progress (Correct or Incorrect).
- "Repeat" Button: Re-play the current audio.
- "Switch Cards" Button: Easily change topics or upload a new file.

The app is completely free and works as a standalone Windows program, via Python, or directly in your web browser. Please feel free to share these links with anyone who might benefit.
